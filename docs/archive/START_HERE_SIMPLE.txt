THE WORKFLOW (SIMPLE!)
----------------------

1. Connect your Google Drive folder:
   stem init --cloud "https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/YOUR_FOLDER_ID"

2. Add files:
   stem add .

3. Commit:
   stem commit -m "Initial commit"

4. Push to Google Drive:
   stem push

5. First time only: Authorize STEM (Google will ask - just click Allow)

6. Push again:
   stem push

DONE! Files uploaded to Google Drive!
